Welcome to the Bark & Ambrosia Beetle Gallery. The world's largest collection of bark and ambrosia beetle images and specimen data. Image Attribution Images in this database were contributed by the global community of entomologists and museum curators. Their names and institutions are listed with each respective file and will be acknowledged in an upcoming publication. Maintained by the University of Florida Forest Entomology Lab.
