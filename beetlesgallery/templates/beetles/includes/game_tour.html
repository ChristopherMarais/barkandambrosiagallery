Spotted a bad photo?

  1. 1Tap Report at the top right of the photo.
  2. 2Pick what's wrong: the box doesn't fit, or it's a bad photo.
  3. 3That's it. You lose no points.

A clear photo that just shows too little isn't bad: name it as far as you can.
