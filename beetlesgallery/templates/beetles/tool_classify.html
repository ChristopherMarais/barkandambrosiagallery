About IBBI-AI

IBBI-AI is our Intelligent Bark Beetle Identifier. Its models come from IBBI, our open-source package for detecting and identifying bark and ambrosia beetles (paper, code). The IBBI documentation lists what each model can name and how accurate it is.

Treat the answer as a screening tool and have important identifications checked by a taxonomist. Images you submit that contain a bark or ambrosia beetle are added to the gallery as unvalidated images, with the proposed names, for experts to check.

Before you use IBBI-AI

IBBI-AI is a screening tool and its answers can be wrong.

What we keep. If IBBI-AI finds a bark or ambrosia beetle in an image you submit, we add that image to the Bark & Ambrosia Beetle Gallery as an unvalidated image, with the proposed labels, so experts can check it. We remove GPS location data from the copy we keep. We do not keep images where no beetle is found, and we do not keep an image we already have.

Your choice. Tick “Don't keep my image” on the page at any time and we will not keep what you submit. You can change your mind for each image. Only submit images you have the right to share, and no images of people.

Kept images may be shown in the gallery and used for research on bark and ambrosia beetles. To have one removed, contact the gallery team.
