Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

The Forest Entomology Lab, University of Florida, runs this website (Bark and Ambrosia Gallery) and is responsible for your personal data here (the "controller").{% if privacy_contact_email %} To ask about your data, write to {{ privacy_contact_email }}. {% endif %}

What we collect

  • Accounts: the username, email address and password you give when you sign up, and what you do in the gallery and the game under your account. This stays on our server. We do not send it to Google.
  • Analytics, only if you accept: the pages you visit (without any IDs or search words), when a game starts, how many answers a round had, and the steps of signing up. Google Analytics also records your IP address, browser and device, and an approximate location.
  • Your cookie choice: one essential cookie that remembers Accept or Reject for a year.

Cookies this site uses

Some cookies keep the site working and are set however you answer the cookie notice ("essential"); one more is set only if you accept, to measure visits ("analytics"). None are used for advertising.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is run by Google. Google processes the analytics data for us as our processor, under Google's data processing terms, which include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and other safeguards for sending data to countries outside the UK and EEA, including the United States. Google does not get your name, your account or anything you type into the site from us.

How long we keep it

  • Analytics data: 14 months in Google Analytics, then it is deleted automatically.
  • Your cookie choice: one year, then you are asked again.
  • Accounts: while the account exists. Ask us to delete it.

Your choice, and how to change it

Analytics runs only with your consent (the legal basis for it), which you give by clicking Accept on the cookie notice. Nothing analytics-related is set before that. You can click Reject instead, and withdrawing is just as easy as giving: open Cookie settings at the bottom of any page, or from "How to play" on the game page, and choose again at any time. Rejecting (or withdrawing) stops analytics straight away and removes the Google Analytics cookies from your browser. Saying no, or changing your mind, never affects your access to the site.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, or for it in a portable format. You can object to analytics, and withdraw your consent, at any time, as described above.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to a data protection authority: your own country's authority if you are in the EU, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you are in the UK, or ours if you tell us first and we will try to put it right.
