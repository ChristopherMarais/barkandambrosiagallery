Privacy notice

Who we are

The Bark and Ambrosia Gallery is the owner of this website. It is responsible for your personal data here (the "controller").{% if privacy_contact_email %} To ask about your data, write to {{ privacy_contact_email }}.{% endif %}

What we collect

  • Accounts: the username, email address and password you give when you sign up, and what you do in the gallery and the game under your account. This stays on our server. We do not send it to Google.
  • Analytics, only if you accept: the pages you visit (without any IDs or search words), when a game starts, how many answers a round had, and the steps of signing up. Google Analytics also records your IP address, browser and device, and an approximate location.
  • Your cookie choice: one essential cookie that remembers Accept or Reject for a year.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is run by Google. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID, so it can tell visits apart. Google processes the analytics data for us as our processor, under Google's data processing terms. Google may process it in other countries, including the United States, with the safeguards those terms provide. Google does not get your name, your account or anything you type into the site from us.

How long we keep it

  • Analytics data: 14 months in Google Analytics.
  • Your cookie choice: one year, then you are asked again.
  • Accounts: while the account exists. Ask us to delete it.

Your choice, and how to change it

We set the analytics cookie only if you click Accept. You can click Reject instead, and you can change your mind at any time: use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page. Rejecting stops analytics straight away and removes the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to limit how we use it. You can object to analytics, and withdraw your consent, at any time, as described above.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your national data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
