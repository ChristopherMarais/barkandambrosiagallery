Bark and Ambrosia Beetle Image Database

Search, upload, and download curated images and specimen data for the world’s bark and ambrosia beetles (Scolytinae and Platypodinae). Created and is maintained by the University of Florida Forest Entomology lab. Records were contributed by Andrew Johnson, Jiri Hulcr, Nathaniel Levia, Tom Atkinson, Averie Kulbeda, many other members the global community of taxonomists, and museum collections. Attributions are noted within individual records. The database also contains photos of TYPE specimens and their labels for approximately a third of the world fauna. (Image: Dinoplatypus javanus by Averie Kulbeda)
